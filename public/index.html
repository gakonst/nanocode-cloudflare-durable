nanocode on Cloudflare.
Durable agents in Durable Objects.

This agent runs in a Cloudflare Durable Object: the nanocode SDK compiled to WebAssembly, with its journal in the object's own SQLite. When the object resets mid-turn, its alarm starts it again, and the turn continues from the last checkpoint. Cloudflare AI Gateway serves its model requests.
